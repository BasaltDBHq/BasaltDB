common-words.txt is derived from the 12dicts word lists, version 6.0.2,
compiled by Alan Beale (http://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts/), list
Lemmatized/2+2+3frq.txt.

Alan Beale releases the 12dicts lists to the public domain and asks for
acknowledgment of their use. The 2+2+3 lists depend on the Automatically
Generated Inflection Database (AGID) by Kevin Atkinson, so its terms apply to
this file too: no further requirements beyond those of AGID and its sources.
Those terms (the AGID copyright and permission notice, and the notices of its
sources: the Moby lists, ENABLE2K, YAWL, the UK Advanced Cryptics Dictionary,
WordNet 1.6 and the Jargon File list) are in agid.txt in this directory,
the AGID documentation as shipped with 12dicts 6.0.2, unchanged but for its
encoding (UTF-8) and line endings.
